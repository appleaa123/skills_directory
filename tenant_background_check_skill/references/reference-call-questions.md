# Landlord reference call questions

Designed to catch a friend posing as a landlord. Ask open questions and let the
reference supply the details.

1. "Which unit did they rent from you, and for what dates?" (compare with the application)
2. "What was the monthly rent, and how was it paid?"
3. "Were there any late payments or notices?"
4. "Why are they leaving?"
5. "Would you rent to them again?"
6. "Do you own the property, or manage it for someone?" (then check public records or listings)
7. "Who else lived in the unit?"
8. Ask for a callback number on the management company's public line, if they say they are a property manager.

Red signs: vague dates, can't name the rent, answers that just echo your
question, reluctance to give a public business number.
