# Workflow: Project Breakdown

**Route here from**: SCATTERED, when the ask names a whole project ("write the report," "plan the move," "redo the resume") rather than a single task.

## The failure mode this avoids

The default response to "help me break this down" is a full outline — 8 to 15 steps covering the entire project end to end. That outline is *correct* and *useless*: it's still too abstract to start, and now there's a wall of steps to feel behind on instead of one vague project. The goal here is not a complete plan. It's finding the **one physical, first action** — everything after that gets figured out once the first thing is moving.

## Procedure

1. **Name the "good enough" done state in one sentence.** Not the ideal outcome — the version that lets this stop being a problem. "A 3-paragraph summary that lets the team proceed" beats "a comprehensive report." A vague project without a done-state is the actual source of the paralysis, more often than the size of the task.

2. **Ask what's actually stopping them right now** — not generically, specifically: "What happens when you try to start this?" The answer usually reveals which category it is:
   - *Don't know where to begin* → go to step 3.
   - *Know what to do, can't make myself do it* → this is STUCK, not SCATTERED — route to [patterns.md#pattern-2](../patterns.md#pattern-2-the-task-atomizer--step-zero-extractor-anti-paralysis) instead.
   - *Too many valid starting points, frozen by choice* → any reasonable starting point works; pick one and say so plainly rather than asking them to choose.

3. **Find the Step Zero** `[practitioner-common]`: the action that takes under 2 minutes, requires no decision, and produces no real progress — its only job is to exist so there's nothing left to "start." *"Create a blank Google Doc and title it 'Project Draft'"* — not "outline the report."

4. **Name only the next visible milestone after Step Zero**, not the whole path. One waypoint, not a map. "After that, the next real chunk is [X]" — stated, not elaborated.

5. **Offer the 5-Minute Launch Contract**: commit to Step Zero plus five minutes, with explicit permission to stop after — see SKILL.md §1.B.

## Ends in

One Step Zero action they can do in the next 60 seconds, and the name of the *next* milestone — not a breakdown of the whole project. If they want the full breakdown after Step Zero is done, that's a new request, made from a position of already having started.
