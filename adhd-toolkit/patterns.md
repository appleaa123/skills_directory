# ADHD Toolkit: Core Execution Patterns & Protocols

Concrete, step-by-step interactive protocols designed for AI assistants and individuals with ADHD to overcome cognitive friction, initiate action, regulate state, and sustain momentum.

> **Operating layer.** These protocols are design decisions for this skill. They are
> *informed* by documented ADHD research and practice but are not procedures any
> specific source publishes verbatim, and they carry no source citations. Where a
> protocol leans on a named community tool, that tool is tagged `[practitioner-common]`.
> The documented material lives in [chapters/](chapters/), [glossary.md](glossary.md),
> and [sources.md](sources.md).
>
> Pattern 6 covers food. It is informational, not medical advice.

## Pattern 1: The Interactive Body-Doubling Sprint (Co-Working Loop)

**When to use**: High task resistance, difficulty staying on task, or loneliness/isolation during administrative or complex work.

### Protocol Steps:
1. **Declare the Single Target (60 seconds)**:
   - State one specific, tangible deliverable: *"During this 20-minute sprint, I am writing the 3 bullet points for section 2."*
   - Explicitly ban multi-tasking: *"Everything else goes to the Parking Lot."*
2. **Set the Visual Countdown**:
   - Set a physical or digital visual timer for 15 or 25 minutes.
   - Start an ambient focus accompaniment (e.g. brown noise or video game soundtrack).
3. **Silent Co-Execution Block**:
   - Work quietly on the declared target.
   - If an unrelated bright idea or urgent urge pops up, jot it on a physical sticky note ("The Parking Lot") and return immediately to the sprint.
4. **Milestone Check-In & Momentum Dopamine**:
   - When the timer rings, stop immediately.
   - Report progress: *"I completed bullet points 1 and 2."*
   - Celebrate completion: Do a quick 60-second stretch or physical high-five.
5. **Decide Next Action**:
   - Take a 3-minute Dopamenu Appetizer `[practitioner-common]`, or start Sprint #2 with a newly declared target.

## Pattern 2: The Task Atomizer & Step-Zero Extractor (Anti-Paralysis) `[practitioner-common]`

**When to use**: Experiencing the "wall of awful," staring at a blank screen, feeling completely frozen or overwhelmed by a vague project.

### Protocol Steps:
1. **Brain Dump (Zero Organization)**:
   - Dictate or type all floating thoughts and worries without filtering or organizing.
2. **Extract the Real Goal in 1 Sentence**:
   - Formulate the "Good Enough" definition of done (e.g. *"A 3-paragraph summary that lets the team proceed"*).
3. **Deconstruct to "Step Zero" (< 2 Minutes)**:
   - What is the absurdly small, physical action that requires zero cognitive effort?
   - *Example*: Instead of *"Write report"*, Step Zero is: *"Create a blank Google Doc and title it 'Project Draft'"*.
4. **The 5-Minute Launch Contract**:
   - Commit only to executing Step Zero and working for 5 minutes.
   - Explicit permission: *"If I still feel terrible after 5 minutes, I am 100% allowed to stop with zero guilt."*
5. **Momentum Capture**:
   - Crossing the activation threshold is usually the hard part; continuing is often easier than starting was.
   - *(An earlier version claimed this works "in 90% of cases." That figure is not documented anywhere in this skill's sources and has been removed.)*

## Pattern 3: The PINCH Motivation Reframing Protocol `[practitioner-common]`

**When to use**: Procrastinating on a dry, repetitive, or uninspiring task that is objectively important.

*PINCH is a community mnemonic. The documented framing is that the ADHD brain engages through "urgency, challenge, novelty, and interest" rather than importance `[verified]`.*

### Protocol Steps:
1. **Identify the Missing Spark**:
   - Acknowledge that the task has no innate neurochemical pull.
2. **Apply PINCH Layering (Select at least 2)**:
   - **P (Passion)**: Connect the task to a person you love or a core value (*"Filing these invoices protects my family's financial freedom"*).
   - **I (Interest)**: Find an odd angle or puzzle (*"How can I write this code using the fewest lines of syntax?"*).
   - **N (Novelty)**: Change location, device, or visual formatting (*"I will work from a coffee shop with a bright fountain pen"*).
   - **C (Competition / Challenge)**: Set a timer for 12 minutes (*"Can I clear 10 items before the bell rings?"*).
   - **H (Hurry / Urgency)**: Create an external mini-deadline (*"I will message my partner that this file is sent by 3:15 PM"*).
3. **Execute Under the Injected Catalyst**.

## Pattern 4: The 3-Minute Cerebellar & DMN/TPN Physical Reset

**When to use**: Trapped in negative self-talk, brain fog, or analysis paralysis (hyperactive Default Mode Network).

### Protocol Steps:
1. **Physical Sever**:
   - Stand up immediately from your chair. Walk away from the screen.
2. **Balance & Vestibular Stimulation (60s)**:
   - Stand on your left leg for 30 seconds with arms outstretched.
   - Switch to right leg for 30 seconds. (Close eyes for added cerebellar demand).
3. **Cross-Lateral Integration (60s)**:
   - Touch right elbow to left knee, then left elbow to right knee in a brisk march (20 reps).
4. **Sensory Reset (60s)**:
   - Drink a glass of cold water with lemon, or splash cold water on face.
5. **Immediate Re-Entry (TPN Anchor)**:
   - Sit back down and immediately execute one single micro-action with your hands.

## Pattern 5: The RSD Emergency Triaging Protocol

**When to use**: Acute emotional flood, panic, or intense defensive feelings triggered by perceived criticism, rejection, or disappointment.

### Protocol Steps:
1. **Label the Neurochemistry**:
   - Say aloud or mentally: *"This is a Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria spike. My nervous system is ringing a false five-alarm fire alarm."*
2. **Activate the Vagus Nerve Brake (60s)** `[practitioner-common]`:
   - Perform 3 Physiological Sighs: Inhale deeply through nose -> sniff an extra burst of air at the top -> long, slow, unforced exhale through mouth.
   - *(The physiological sigh comes from respiratory-physiology research, not from this skill's documented sources.)*
3. **Separate Data from Story (The 2-Column Split)**:
   - *Column A (Raw Video Camera Data)*: What literally happened/what was verbatim said.
   - *Column B (The RSD Narrative)*: What my brain is telling me it means about my worth.
4. **Enforce the 24-Hour Cooling Runway**:
   - Do NOT send an email, text, or make a decision. Draft the response in a detached notepad if needed, but do not send.
5. **Reach for Vitamin Connect** `[verified]`:
   - Contact a safe person who knows your ADHD or review your pre-recorded voice note/success list.

## Pattern 6: The ADHD Focus Plate & Micronutrient Stacking Protocol

**When to use**: Planning meals to maintain sustained dopamine and avoid afternoon cognitive crashes.

### Protocol Steps:
1. **Anchor with Bioavailable Protein**:
   - Pasture-raised eggs, wild salmon, organic chicken, or hemp/pumpkin seed protein.
   - Gut bacteria synthesize precursors such as phenylalanine that the body uses to build dopamine and noradrenaline `[verified]`. The specific 25–35g target is `[practitioner-common]`, not a documented figure.
2. **Add Complex Prebiotic Fiber & Polyphenols**:
   - Handful of wild blueberries or blackberries, dark leafy greens (kale/spinach), or roasted asparagus.
   - Nourishes gut microbiota to synthesize dopamine and GABA while preventing blood sugar spikes.
3. **Incorporate Neuro-Protective Fats**:
   - Half an avocado, 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil, or a handful of raw walnuts (rich in plant Omega-3s).
4. **Hydrate with Mineral Electrolytes**:
   - 500ml filtered water with a pinch of sea salt and squeeze of fresh lemon.
5. **Consider the documented avoid-list** `[verified]`:
   - Gluten if you have celiac disease or non-celiac gluten sensitivity; A1 milk caseins (prefer A2, nut, goat's or sheep's milk); caffeine above 400 mg/day.
   - Food colorings and additives are worth eliminating **only if symptoms don't respond to gentler changes** — meta-analyses estimate 10–30% of people with ADHD respond.
   - The documented evidence is explicit that **sugar does not cause ADHD**, though limiting it for general health is still recommended.
