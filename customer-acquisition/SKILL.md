# Customer Acquisition

An operating playbook for turning a business into a machine that reliably produces interested
prospects — more of them, better-fit, cheaper, from several sources.

## When to use this

- Lead flow is too low, or unpredictable month to month
- You're starting outreach, content, or paid advertising
- A channel that was working has plateaued
- You need a lead magnet to convert cold traffic
- You want a daily prospecting routine that actually gets done
- You want customers, staff, agencies, or partners generating leads for you

## The core model

**Chase engaged prospects, not raw contacts.** A *contact* is anyone you can reach. An *engaged
prospect* is a contact who has shown interest — replied, clicked, booked, opted in. Engaged
prospects are the real output of advertising, and the only thing worth counting.
→ `plays/define-your-lead.md`

**Grow a business two ways:** get more customers, or make each worth more. This playbook is the
first — via lead flow that is *more, better-fit, cheaper, and reliable (from several sources)*.
Roughly: double your leads, double your business.

**Turn contacts into engaged prospects** by advertising your core offer directly (try this
first) or, if that's not enough, with a **lead magnet** — a complete solution to a narrow
problem, free or cheap, that reveals the next problem your core offer solves. → `plays/lead-magnets.md`

**There are only four ways to let people know about what you sell** — two audiences × two
delivery modes:

| | Warm (they gave permission) | Cold (strangers) |
|---|---|---|
| **One-to-one (private)** | warm outreach | cold outreach |
| **One-to-many (public)** | free content | paid ads |

If you aren't getting enough leads, you're not running these four with enough **skill** or
enough **volume**. → `plays/warm-outreach.md`, `plays/content.md`, `plays/cold-outreach.md`, `plays/paid-ads.md`

**Scale a channel in three moves, in order:** do far more of it → fix the biggest drop-off point
→ expand to new placements, platforms, and channels. → `plays/scale-a-channel.md`

**Get force multipliers** — customers, staff, agencies, and partners who run the four channels
*for* you, and who can recruit more of the same. → `plays/referrals.md`, `plays/hiring-for-outreach.md`, `plays/agencies.md`, `plays/affiliates.md`

**Execute daily.** Commit to a volume of prospecting actions every day, or to an outcome number
you hit no matter how long it takes, planned on a single page. → `plays/daily-advertising-plan.md`

## Scope

A general operating playbook for lead generation and direct-response advertising. It assumes you
already have a solid core offer (see `offer-design`) and a way to monetize customers over time
(see `offer-monetization`). Numeric guidance is a starting heuristic; check outreach, data, and
advertising tactics against current law and platform terms.
