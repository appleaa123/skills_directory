# Growth Stages

## When to use it
To locate where your advertising currently operates, so you know the next move. Each stage has a
different constraint.

## The method

The advertiser's growth ladder: six stages, each with a different constraint and a different
primary action.

## The stages

**Stage 1 — Your network knows.** One offer, one audience, one platform. The moment you get
engaged prospects, you can make money.
*Primary action: warm outreach.*

**Stage 2 — You consistently reach everyone you can.** You know the exact inputs that produce an
engaged prospect and you scale them to your personal capacity. Best to lean on warm outreach and
free content here.
*Primary actions: as much warm outreach and content as you can sustain.*

**Stage 3 — Staff help you advertise.** Your personal inputs are maxed but the platform isn't.
Hire people to run channels profitably on your behalf.
*Primary action: hire people to advertise profitably for you.*

**Stage 4 — The product is good enough for consistent referrals.** Build goodwill; aim for 25%+
of customers from referrals. Update the product on a fast cadence from customer feedback; run a
strong referral program. **This is where most businesses fail — they let the product slip while
scaling and never recover.**
*Primary action: fix the product until referrals are consistent, then resume scaling with a
bigger team.*

**Stage 5 — More places, more ways, more people.** Expand to new audiences on your best
platform, then use all its placements and formats, then add another platform, multiplier, or
channel.
*Primary action: advertise profitably with 2+ methods across multiple platforms.*

**Stage 6 — Experienced leaders run channels without you.** Hire proven specialists (not
potential) to grow whole departments around a channel or platform, with strong incentives.
*Primary action: get battle-tested leaders to own channels.*

## Steps
1. Match your situation to a stage by its primary action.
2. Do that stage's primary action until the constraint moves.
3. Don't skip Stage 4 — referrals are the flywheel that lets you scale without ads being your
   only source.

## Pitfalls
- Scaling ad spend (Stage 5) while the product quietly degrades (Stage 4 undone).
- Hiring for "potential" at Stage 6 instead of proven specialists.

## Metrics & targets
- Stage 4 gate: ≥25% of customers from referrals before scaling the team again.
