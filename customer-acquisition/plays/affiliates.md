# Affiliates and Partners

## When to use it
The highest-leverage multiplier, once your product and referral engine are solid. Other
businesses promote you to *their* audiences for a cut — and they can recruit more partners.

## The method
Two models:

1. **Partner gives away your lead magnet** bundled with their own offer. Your lead magnet makes
   their offer more valuable, so they can charge more and get more leads — and you get the
   prospects it produces.
   - *Sample/trial:* a massage business partners with the gym next door — every new gym member
     gets a free massage. Stronger gym offer; massage leads for you.
   - *Reveal a problem:* a free assessment bundled with the partner's package; you then make an
     offer to fix what you found.
   - *One step of a process:* the partner gives away step one of your multi-step solution; you
     upsell the rest.
2. **Partner sells your lead magnet** (a workshop, sample, mini-service). Let them **keep 100%
   of that revenue** — it's pure profit and no work for them, so they send you more prospects.
   You earn on the core-offer upsell afterward, with no split owed on it.

Flow: partner sells their core offer → gives or sells your lead magnet → you upsell your core
offer and everything after.

Partners need three things to succeed: a great lead magnet, **training** on how to promote it,
and **fast, reliable payouts.** Recruit partners using the four channels.

## Steps
1. Package a lead magnet that strengthens partners' offers.
2. Choose the model: bundled give-away, or partner-sells-and-keeps-the-cash.
3. Provide the magnet, a short training, and dependable payouts.
4. Recruit partners with the four channels; help each launch.

## Example
A supplement brand runs an affiliate program: partner gyms give every new member a free
nutrition consult delivered by the brand's team. The gym markets the added value and charges
more; the brand upsells products during the consult. Everyone profits.

## Pitfalls
- Making it work-heavy for the partner instead of "pure profit, no work."
- Launching without training or reliable payouts.

## Metrics & targets
- Partners can recruit more partners — do the work once, the leads keep coming.
