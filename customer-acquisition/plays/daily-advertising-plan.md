# The Daily Prospecting Plan

## When to use it
Every working day. Reading about lead generation doesn't produce leads — prospecting does. This
is the execution layer.

## The method

### Volume commitment: 100 actions a day for 100 days
Do 100 primary prospecting actions per day, every day, for 100 days straight. Per channel:
- **Warm outreach:** 100 reach-outs/day.
- **Content:** 100 minutes/day making it; publish at least one piece daily.
- **Cold outreach:** 100 reach-outs/day; expect low response, use automation.
- **Paid ads:** 100 minutes/day making ads; run them for 100 days.

**Scale the number to your channel and deal size.** 100 is the anchor, not a universal rule —
what matters is a fixed, non-negotiable daily count that you actually hit. For a high-ticket,
low-volume motion (enterprise deals, expensive services) 20–40 genuinely researched, personal
touches a day may be the right commitment; for high-volume cold outreach or content, 100+ is
normal. Pick the highest number you can sustain every working day and hold it.

### Outcome-locked blocks (the version for scale)
Instead of committing to an action count, commit to an **outcome number** and work until it's
hit — no matter how long it takes. Some days that's 50 actions; some days it's far more. It
unlocks effort you didn't know you had.

A supporting structure: a long, uninterrupted work block right after a full night's sleep, no
meetings until it's done, then everything else. Set the daily goal from what you know you can
produce in that block.

### One-page plan (5 steps)
1. Pick the prospect type to get: customers, partners, staff, or agencies.
2. Pick the volume commitment or the outcome commitment. Commit to the daily action.
3. Fill out a one-page checklist for that daily action.
4. Do it daily until you can afford to pay someone else to.
5. Then go back to step 1 with *staff* as the target, and repeat — then scale again.

## Steps
1. Choose one channel and one daily action.
2. Set the daily count — 100 as the anchor, adjusted to your channel and deal size — or a daily
   outcome number. Commit to it for ~100 days.
3. Protect one uninterrupted work block for it.
4. Do it daily; hand it off when you can afford to; repeat for the next layer.

## Example
A founder commits to 100 cold emails a day for 100 days, written in a protected 7–10am block,
tracked on one page. By week 6 the reply-to-call rate is predictable enough to hire a
part-time SDR and shift the founder's 100 to recruiting.

## Pitfalls
- A hundred-page plan that never gets used — one page, filled in five minutes, leaves no room
  for excuses.
- "Doing your best" instead of doing what the number requires.

## Metrics & targets
- A fixed daily count (100 as the anchor, scaled to your channel and deal size) × ~100 days, or
  a fixed daily outcome.
- One page. You either did the actions or you didn't.
