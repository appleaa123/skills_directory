# Patterns — Customer Acquisition

## Lead magnet build (7 steps)
**When:** direct core-offer advertising underperforms; expensive or complex product.
**How:** pick a narrow, meaningful problem + who → solve it (reveal-a-problem / sample-trial /
one-step) → decide delivery → test the name → make it easy to consume → make it genuinely good →
make it easy to say "I want more."
**Trade-offs:** must stand alone in value *and* reveal a problem your core offer closes.

## The four outreach channels

### Warm outreach (1:1, warm)
**When:** day one; cheapest, highest response; any time you need leads fast.
**How:** list everyone contactable → reconnect value-first, no ask → 100 personalized
reach-outs/day, up to 3 touches → acknowledge–affirm–ask on every reply, toward your offer.
**Trade-offs:** caps at your network size; exhaust it, then go cold.

### Free content (1:many, warm)
**When:** to build the compounding asset (the audience).
**How:** every piece = get-noticed → keep-watching → pay-off. Topics from distant past / recent
past / present / trending / demonstrations. Give far more than you ask. Publish as much as you
can sustain.
**Trade-offs:** slow to compound; effort leads results by months.

### Cold outreach (1:1, cold)
**When:** you want to choose exactly who you target; warm audience exhausted.
**How:** build a targeted list (software → brokers → manual; test before scaling) → personalize
+ big fast value → volume, channel variety, follow-ups, automation.
**Trade-offs:** low response rate; a numbers game; check platform/privacy rules.

### Paid ads (1:many, cold)
**When:** to scale reach fast, once you can fund the learning curve.
**How:** attention hook (load the first seconds; labels; local + type of person) → value
(benefit / stakeholders / timeline) → explicit CTA. Fund via customer-funded acquisition; test
to ~2× first-30-day cash, kill no-lead ads before 1×; once break-even, reverse budget from
customer capacity (+20%).
**Trade-offs:** costs money to make profitable; measure over a long horizon.

## The four force multipliers

### Customer referrals
**When:** build first — you already get customers via the four channels. Target ≥25% of new
customers.
**How:** earn goodwill by giving more value (better-fit customers, better expectations, better/
faster results, less effort, clear next step); add an incentivized program; ask at peak
goodwill.
**Trade-offs:** depends entirely on product quality — protect it.

### Hiring for outreach
**When:** your personal prospecting capacity is maxed.
**How:** run the "internal" version of the four channels to recruit (network, recruiting, job
posts, promoted posts; plus employee referrals, professional groups, staffing firms). Hand off
one channel at a time.
**Trade-offs:** retention is the real constraint — fix churn before scaling headcount.

### Agencies
**When:** you have money and want to learn a new channel/platform fast.
**How:** hire as paid training — a fixed period to learn it, extra pay to explain decisions,
then step down to advisory. Don't rent forever.
**Trade-offs:** their model wants you dependent; no money → learn by trial and error instead.

### Affiliates & partners
**When:** highest leverage; after product and referrals are solid.
**How:** partner gives your lead magnet bundled with their offer, or sells it and keeps 100% of
that revenue (you earn on the core-offer upsell). Provide magnet + training + reliable payouts.
Recruit with the four channels.
**Trade-offs:** infrastructure-heavy to launch.

## Scaling a channel (three moves, in order)
1. **Volume** — 10× the volume of what works (100 actions × 100 days) until cost per acquisition
   breaks.
2. **Optimization** — hold that volume; find the biggest drop-off, test until it frees, repeat
   on the next.
3. **New surface** — when volume and optimization returns fall below a new option: new placement
   → new platform → new channel.

## Daily execution
- A fixed daily count for ~100 days (100 anchor, scaled to channel and deal size — high-ticket
  B2B may be 20–40 personalized touches/day), or an outcome-locked block (commit to a number,
  work until done).
- One-page plan: pick prospect type → pick volume/outcome commitment → fill the checklist → do
  it daily until you can hire it out → repeat with "staff" as the target.
