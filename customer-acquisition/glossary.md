# Glossary — Customer Acquisition

**Acknowledge–affirm–ask** — a reply pattern: restate what they said, give a genuine compliment,
then ask a question steering toward your offer.

**Attention hook** — whatever makes the right person notice an ad in the first few seconds;
ranges from hyper-specific to broad. Labels (words a group identifies with) work well.

**Big fast value** — overwhelming a cold prospect with a useful result, resource, or insight
before any ask, to overcome lack of trust.

**Contact** — a person you can reach. Nothing more.

**Customer-funded acquisition** — using the cash a customer generates in the first ~30 days to
pay for acquiring them (and more).

**Engaged prospect** — a contact who has shown interest in what you sell; the true output of
advertising.

**Force multiplier** — someone who runs the four outreach channels for you: customers,
staff, agencies, partners. Higher leverage than doing it yourself; can recruit more of the same.

**The four outreach channels** — the only ways to let people know about what you sell: warm
outreach (1:1, warm), free content (1:many, warm), cold outreach (1:1, cold), paid ads (1:many,
cold).

**Get-noticed / keep-watching / pay-off** — the three jobs of any content unit.

**Lead magnet** — a complete solution to a narrow problem, free or cheap, valuable enough to
charge for, that reveals a problem your core offer solves. Three types: reveal a problem,
sample/trial, one step of a multi-step process.

**Market-size illusion** — assuming your niche is the tiny slice you currently advertise to; the
real market is far larger.

**One-page prospecting plan** — pick a prospect type, pick a volume or outcome commitment, fill
one checklist, do it daily until you can hire it out, then repeat for the next layer.

**Outcome-locked block** — committing to an outcome number and working until it's hit, no matter
how long that takes.

**Scaling a channel** — the three-move sequence: do far more volume → fix the biggest drop-off
point → expand to new placements/platforms/channels.

**100 actions × 100 days** — 100 primary prospecting actions per day for 100 days straight.

**Value angles (benefit / stakeholders / timeline)** — the three lenses for ad copy: how it
moves each value driver, everyone whose opinion affects the buyer's standing, and past/present/
future consequences.
