# Cheatsheet — Customer Acquisition

## The goal
**Engaged prospects** (shown interest), not raw contacts. Optimize for more + better-fit +
cheaper + reliable (several sources). Double your leads ≈ double your business.

## Which channel?

| Situation | Channel |
|---|---|
| Day one / need leads this week / no budget | Warm outreach |
| Build a long-term compounding asset | Free content |
| Want to choose exactly who you target | Cold outreach |
| Scale reach fast, can fund a learning curve | Paid ads |
| Not enough leads, period | You're not running the four channels with enough **skill** or **volume** |

## Which force multiplier?

| Situation | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| You already get customers via the four channels | Customer referrals (target ≥25%) |
| Your personal prospecting time is maxed | Hire people to run outreach |
| Need to learn a new channel/platform, have money | Agency (as paid training, then internalize) |
| Product + referrals solid, want max leverage | Affiliates & partners |

## Daily targets
- **A fixed daily count for ~100 days straight — 100 is the anchor, scaled to channel and deal size.**
  - Outreach (warm or cold): 100/day for high-volume; 20–40 personalized touches/day for high-ticket B2B.
  - Content: 100 minutes/day + publish ≥1/day.
  - Paid ads: 100 minutes/day + run ~100 days.
- **Outcome-locked block:** pick the number, work until it's hit.
- The count matters less than never missing a day.

## Paid-ads thresholds
- Metric: lifetime gross profit ÷ cost to acquire.
- Testing a new ad that IS producing leads → let spend run to ~2× first-30-day cash before
  killing.
- New ad producing NO leads → kill before 1× that cash.
- Break-even or better → stop asking "how much to spend?", ask "how many customers can I
  handle?" Reverse budget from that, pad +20%.
- Expect to lose money for months building the machine; judge over a long horizon.

## Ad anatomy
**Attention hook** (first few seconds, most of the effort; labels; local + type of person) →
**value** (benefit / stakeholders / timeline angles) → **CTA** (spell it out: click, call,
reply YES, scan).

## Content anatomy
**Get noticed** → **keep watching** (lists, steps, stories) → **pay off**. Give many times per
ask.

## Reply anatomy
**Acknowledge** (restate) → **affirm** (genuine compliment) → **ask** (steer toward your offer).

## Scaling a channel (three moves, in order)
1. More volume until it breaks. 2. Hold, fix the biggest drop-off, test until it frees, repeat.
3. New surface: placement → platform → channel, only when the first two stop paying off better
than a new option would.
**Market-size illusion:** your niche is far bigger than the slice you advertise to now.

## Growth stages (find yours → next move)
1. Network knows → warm outreach. 2. Consistent, personal capacity → warm + content. 3. Hire
people to advertise. 4. **Fix the product → 25%+ referrals** (most businesses fail here). 5. More
places/ways/people, 2+ methods on multiple platforms. 6. Hire proven leaders to own channels.
