# Glossary — Offer Monetization

**Attraction offer** — a free or discounted offer that turns strangers into customers and
converts advertising into cash.

**Basic-vs-premium side-by-side** — advertise a cheap/free version, then present it next to a
premium version and emphasize the premium; close both types of buyer.

**Buy more, get some free** — free units or time given in exchange for paid purchases; works
better with more free than paid.

**Card on file** — a stored payment method that removes friction from the next offer.

**Commitment fee waiver** — a startup fee (3–5× monthly) waived for a term commitment; cancel
early and you owe the fee.

**Conditional free trial** — a free trial contingent on meeting stated terms; miss them and you
pay a penalty fee. For recurring products where the customer must do work.

**Continuity offer** — ongoing value for ongoing payments until the customer cancels.

**Credit-forward offer** — crediting a customer's prior spend toward the next offer; used to win
back lapsed or unhappy customers.

**Downsell offer** — an offer made after a "no" that changes price or terms so the buyer can say
yes.

**Feature-reduction downsell** — lowering the price by removing components, highest value first,
so the contrast reveals what was removed.

**Four-week billing** — billing every 4 weeks instead of monthly → 13 cycles a year → about
+8.3% revenue for the same work.

**Free-time subscription discount** — free subscription time (now, later, or spread) for signing
up today.

**Guided-menu upsell** — de-select what the customer doesn't need, prescribe what they do, ask A
vs. B, offer the card on file.

**Next-problem upsell** — offering the solution to the customer's next problem the moment they
become aware of it; "you can't have X without Y."

**Payment plan** — same product, same total price, paid in installments timed to the customer's
pay dates.

**Pay now or pay later** — a choice between a $0 card-on-file "pay later, only if satisfied" and
a discounted "pay now" with bonuses.

**Price-anchored step-down** — present the most expensive option first, then offer a much
cheaper alternative with the same core function.

**Prize giveaway offer** — a contest for a grand prize (= your core offer) in exchange for
contact info; sell the non-winners at a discount.

**Results-based refund offer** — a deposit refunded (as cash or credit) if the customer hits the
goal, does the actions, or both.

**Sign-up-bonus subscription** — a members-only bonus worth more than the first payment, given
for starting the subscription today.

**Stages (get cash / get more cash / get the most cash)** — attraction → upsell + downsell →
continuity; build one at a time.

**30-day payback rule** — gross profit from a customer must exceed the cost to get and serve
them within about 30 days (minimum); enough for 2+ more customers means cash no longer limits
growth.

**Upsell offer** — an offer that gets a customer to spend more, immediately.
