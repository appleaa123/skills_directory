# Next-Problem Upsell (upsell)

## When to use it
Whenever your core offer solves one problem and creates or exposes another. The single most
profitable and easiest upsell — and if you sell only one thing, building this is your
highest-leverage move.

## The method
Offer the solution to the customer's **next problem the moment they become aware of it** —
which is usually right at the point of purchase. Framing: **"you can't have X without Y."**

It works because your existing customer converts far better than a stranger, and because the
next problem is genuinely real and genuinely theirs. You need to understand the customer's
problem sequence better than they do.

## Steps
1. Map the chain of problems your core offer creates or exposes.
2. Package the solution to the immediate next one.
3. Present it at the moment of purchase, in "can't have X without Y" terms.
4. Make sure it raises 30-day cash — that's the metric this funds.

## Example
A checkout flow for a standing desk: desk → cable-management tray ("you'll want this or the
cables hang") → anti-fatigue mat → monitor arm. Each add-on solves a problem that only becomes
obvious once the desk is in the cart.

## Pitfalls
- Selling only one thing — a business with nothing to sell after the first purchase is leaving
  most of its revenue on the table.
- Forcing "can't have X without Y" where the next problem isn't real.
- Timing the offer long after purchase, once the problem's urgency has faded.

## Metrics & targets
- Current customers always convert better than strangers — this is the cheapest sale you'll make.
- Each upsell should push 30-day gross profit further above acquisition + service cost.
