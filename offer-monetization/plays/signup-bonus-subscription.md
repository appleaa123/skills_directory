# Sign-Up-Bonus Subscription (continuity)

## When to use it
To convert front-end buyers into recurring members.

## The method
Give the customer a strong bonus **if they start the subscription today** — typically a bonus
worth more than the first recurring payment, so joining feels like being paid to subscribe.

Point-of-sale sequence: run the normal pitch → "want it for free?" (yes) → "become a member and
it's free" → "and members also get member-only extras" (better access, events, perks) → then
upsell a discounted prepaid term.

The bonuses are **members-only** — they don't exist outside the subscription, which is what
pulls people into recurring billing rather than the one-time purchase.

## Steps
1. Make the sign-up bonus worth more than the first payment.
2. Make the key perks members-only so they can't be obtained any other way.
3. Chain it: free-with-membership → member-only extras → prepaid discount upsell.

## Example
A studio markets a 6-week program but, on the sales floor, pivots: "Want it free? Become a
member and it's free — plus members get priority booking and quarterly member events." Then:
"Want to save more? Prepay 6 months for a discount and an extra perk." Direct-to-membership
conversions rise sharply versus selling the program first.

## Pitfalls
- A sign-up bonus worth less than the first payment.
- Bonuses that are also available to non-members.

## Metrics & targets
- Sign-up bonus value > first recurring payment.
