# Prize Giveaway Offer (attraction)

## When to use it
When you want a large number of pre-qualified leads who have just raised their hand for your
most expensive product.

## The method
Advertise a chance to win a big prize in exchange for contact information (and any other criteria
you want). Pick a winner, then offer everyone else the prize at a discount.

Six steps:
1. **Pick a grand prize = the thing you want everyone to buy.** Attach a monetary value as a
   price anchor.
2. **Pick the consolation offer** — the discounted deal for non-winners (call it a scholarship,
   gift card, credit, voucher).
3. **Ask for contact info** plus eligibility questions ("do you own a clinic?", "why should you
   be selected?").
4. **Set qualifying actions** that also spread the giveaway or show higher intent (share it,
   tag someone, answer questions, attend a call).
5. **Put it on a deadline** — draw in 3–7 days. Update entrants daily with a countdown plus
   value (prize benefits, social proof).
6. **Announce the winner publicly, then privately contact everyone else** — "you qualified for
   something" — with a second short deadline to redeem.

Set the consolation discount around 10–30% of your gross margin. Because the prize carries a
stated value well above its price, even a small discount reads as a large value gap.

## Steps
1. Choose the prize (your core offer) and its anchor value.
2. Define entry: contact info + eligibility + qualifying actions.
3. Run the countdown, draw a winner, then work the non-winner list — that's where the revenue is.

## Example
A dental practice: grand prize = a full set of clear aligners ($6,000 value). Entrants give
contact details and answer two fit questions. After the draw, every non-winner is offered a
$2,000 aligner credit, valid one week — framed as "we picked you for a partial award."

## Pitfalls
- Not planning the non-winner follow-up before launch — the follow-up is the whole point.
- A prize that isn't what you actually sell.

## Metrics & targets
- Consolation discount ≈ 10–30% of gross margin.
- Deadline 3–7 days; second (redemption) deadline ~7 days.
