# Results-Based Refund Offer (attraction)

## When to use it
An entry offer for things people start and quit — fitness, learning a skill, starting a
business, a beauty or health routine. Works on new, current, and lapsed customers.

## The method
The customer pays a deposit. They get it back — as cash or store credit — if they hit the goal,
do the required actions, or both. Structure: **do [X] within [timeframe] under [rules], and it's
free.**

Three qualifying variants:
- **Results:** get the outcome (lose the weight, hit the revenue number) → refund. They bet on
  themselves.
- **Actions:** complete the actions (attend every session, log progress, submit homework) →
  refund regardless of outcome. They bet on following directions.
- **Actions + results:** both required. They bet that following your directions produces the
  outcome — usually the best structure, since most people can't hit a goal without guidance.

It's the steepest possible discount (100%), so it pulls hard. It still makes money: most people
don't complete every requirement, and many who earn the refund reinvest it.

## Steps
1. Pick the goal and the required actions. Make both trivially easy to track.
2. Set the deposit and the refund form (cash or credit).
3. Build every required touchpoint (calls, check-ins) as an opportunity to make the next offer.
4. Reuse these exact criteria later for a conditional free trial (`conditional-free-trial`).

## Example
An online course: "$400 deposit, fully refunded if you finish all 6 modules, post a weekly
update in the group, and attend the final call." Completion rates rise because of the deposit;
finishers who get the refund often roll it into the advanced program.

## Pitfalls
- Fuzzy, hard-to-verify criteria — the whole thing collapses without clean tracking.
- Treating the refund as a loss rather than a completion incentive.

## Metrics & targets
- Best fit: offerings people are known to start and abandon.
- Use with new customers (max discount), current customers (mix them in), and lapsed customers
  (bigger incentive to return).
